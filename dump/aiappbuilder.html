The AI Application Builder is one of UnifyApps' core products and continues to evolve as new capabilities are introduced. Designed for Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs), Product Managers, and Business Analysts, the platform enables enterprise applications to be created through visual workflows instead of traditional development. As the product expanded, the challenge was to keep the experience powerful without increasing complexity.


As a Product Designer, I worked on simplifying how these teams build applications by redesigning key workflows, improving information architecture, and introducing AI-assisted experiences that reduced repetitive configuration. I also designed reusable interaction patterns and expanded the design system, ensuring new capabilities could be added consistently as the product continued to grow.


My focus was on reducing the learning curve while giving users the flexibility to build complex enterprise applications efficiently. By simplifying high-frequency workflows and creating scalable interaction patterns, I helped make the builder faster to learn, easier to navigate, and better suited for an evolving enterprise platform.

Rethinking How Enterprise Applications Are Built

Rethinking How Enterprise Applications Are Built

As AI became a bigger focus for the product, the vision was to bring it directly into the application builder as a natural part of the creation experience. I worked on designing a conversational workflow where users could create and refine enterprise applications through natural language while seeing updates reflected on the canvas in real time. Users could seamlessly move between chatting with the AI and editing the application directly on the canvas, with both experiences remaining fully synchronized. To further streamline the workflow, I introduced contextual references that allow users to bring existing pages, components, and data sources into the conversation, creating a faster and more connected application-building experience.

Managing Multiple Applications from a Single Source

Managing Multiple Applications from a Single Source

As enterprise customers expanded across multiple brands and business units, maintaining separate applications became difficult and time-consuming. I worked on designing App Variants, enabling teams to create multiple branded versions of a single application while keeping the core experience, logic, and workflows shared. Each variant could maintain its own identity through customized themes, assets, and configurations, allowing teams to manage multiple applications from one central source. This approach significantly reduced duplication, simplified maintenance, and made it easier to scale applications across an organization.

Simplifying Releases Without Slowing Down Teams

Simplifying Releases Without Slowing Down Teams

Releasing an application is often a complex process involving multiple tools and manual coordination. I designed App Releases to bring the entire release workflow into the builder, allowing teams to build, test, and deploy applications from a single place. Step-by-step guidance and contextual documentation help users complete each stage with confidence, while a centralized dashboard provides complete visibility into release history, active versions, and deployment status. To keep teams productive, users can continue editing their application while a release is being processed, with version history ensuring every change remains traceable and easy to manage.

Understanding the Impact Before Every Change

Understanding the Impact Before Every Change

As applications became more complex, understanding the impact of a single change became increasingly challenging. I designed a dependency visualization that gives users a complete view of both incoming and outgoing relationships for the selected item. Instead of manually tracing references across the application, users can instantly understand what depends on it, what it depends on, and confidently make changes without introducing unintended issues.

Scaling Design Systems Beyond Themes

Scaling Design Systems Beyond Themes

As organizations built applications for different brands and business units, simple color themes were no longer enough. I worked on designing a comprehensive Theme Manager that allows teams to customize the visual language of an application while keeping it on a single, maintainable foundation. Beyond colors and typography, users can configure icon sets, brand assets, and even customize the appearance of every supported design system component—from buttons and input fields to cards, snackbars, and other reusable UI elements. By separating branding from application logic, teams can create multiple branded experiences without duplicating components or maintaining separate design systems.

What I Learned

What I Learned

Working on UnifyApps fundamentally changed how I think about product design. Unlike traditional applications, every feature had to support highly configurable enterprise workflows while remaining intuitive for Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs), Product Managers, and Business Analysts. This pushed me to think beyond individual screens and focus on systems, scalability, and long-term maintainability.

Designing AI-assisted experiences taught me that successful AI products are not just about generating results, they're about creating predictable, transparent, and collaborative workflows where users remain in control. Features such as conversational application creation, dependency visualization, release management, and scalable theme customization reinforced the importance of balancing powerful capabilities with clarity and ease of use.

Most importantly, this project strengthened my ability to simplify technically complex problems into experiences that feel approachable, enabling teams to build, customize, and manage enterprise applications with greater confidence and efficiency.
